Sole Creator – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Sole Creator is a private tool used only by Sole Miralimov to publish videos to his own YouTube channel and Instagram account and to read their statistics. It is not offered to anyone else.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission (Google OAuth), it uploads videos, thumbnails and captions to the owner's YouTube channel and reads that channel's analytics (views, watch time, audience totals). It does not access any other user's data.

Where data is kept

Access tokens and analytics are stored only on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Google API Services

Sole Creator's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. It uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
